How to Play Questfall: Quest Miner

Master quest mining, interactive mini-games, and character progression.

🎯 1. The Core Mining Loop

Complete quests from the feed to earn Gold (🪙) and XP (⚡). Level up through Leagues to scale your bounties, spend Gold to upgrade character attributes, and crack open Ancient Mining Crates for rare relics.

⛏️ 2. Action Quests & Mini-Games (Costs Stamina)

Action quests trigger one of 7 randomized interactive verification games:

  • Witnessing Match: Scan two 3x3 symbol matrices. Click [MATCH] if identical or [FAKE] if 1 symbol is altered.
  • Precision Mining Bar: An oscillator needle swings back and forth. Click [MINE NOW!] or tap Spacebar. Hit the center green zone for a 2x Critical Gold payout!
  • Honeypot Sweeper: Fast reflex test. Click all 3 green Verified Badges before time runs out. Avoid red Bot / Honeypot traps!
  • Sequence Decrypt: Memorize the 4-step sequence as it flashes with audio tones, then repeat the sequence using 1-4, W/A/S/D, or screen buttons.
  • Resonance Frequency Tuner: Slide the frequency needle or use ← / → or A / D into the green harmonic band to lock resonance and shatter the geode.
  • Overclock Heat Venting: 3 valve chambers rise in pressure. Vent with buttons or 1 / 2 / 3 when chambers hit the 80%–95% Hot Zone to prevent blowouts (100%) and earn a 2x Perfect Vent Combo!
  • Proof-of-Work Hash Sieve: Inspect the cryptographic block feed. Click or press 1-8 to mine 3 Golden Nonce blocks starting with #00 without clicking invalid hashes!

🧠 3. Knowledge Quizzes (Free · 0 Stamina)

Answer trivia questions directly based on the official Questfall Whitepaper & Elysium Blockchain documentation. Correct answers grant instant Gold, XP, and unlock verified architectural insights!

📊 4. Community Surveys (Free · 0 Stamina)

Cast your vote on decentralized governance, RPG economy priorities, and whitelist security to receive immediate consensus stipends.

⚡ 5. Stamina, Attributes & Crates

  • Stamina: Max 1,000. Recharges continuously and completely refills on Level Up!
  • Mining Power: Increases all Gold yields by +15% per upgrade.
  • Recovery Rate: Accelerates stamina recharge (+2/s per level).
  • Luck & Fortune: Increases critical strike chance and high-tier crate drops.
  • Ancient Mining Crates (100g): Yields large Gold caches, Stamina Elixirs (+400 Stamina), or permanent Stat Relics!
